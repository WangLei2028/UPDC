## Publication citation policy

For scholarly or other publication use - including papers, conference
proceedings, theses/dissertations, technical reports, data-release papers, or
other published work - that uses UPDC code, its algorithmic implementation, or
scientific results produced with UPDC, the UPDC project requires citation of
**both** publications:

1. Wang, L., Li, G., & Kang, X. 2022, *Towards super-resolution via iterative
   multi-exposure coaddition*, MNRAS, 517, 787,
   doi:10.1093/mnras/stac2664.
2. Wang, L., Shan, H., Nie, L., et al. 2025, *UPdec-Webb: A Data Set for
   Coaddition of JWST NIRCam Images*, ApJS, 276, 36,
   doi:10.3847/1538-4365/ad9566.

See `CITATION.cff` and `CITATION.md` for machine-readable metadata and BibTeX.

The publication citation policy is a scientific attribution policy of the
project. It is separate from, and does not modify, the MIT License.
